# Naming & Branding Recommendations

This page documents the naming and branding recommendations proposed for the platform during its transition from **Jerb** to **ProcessJR**.

---

## Brand Positioning

> "The operating system for organizational processes and institutional knowledge."

This is the strongest strategic positioning for the platform, balancing enterprise utility with modern operational intelligence.

---

## Name Candidates & Evaluation

Based on the platform's product direction, here is a list of candidate names and their strategic ranking:

|Rank |     Name      | Why                                                                 |
|:--- | :------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | ProcessOS     | Best enterprise positioning; sounds like a core infrastructure layer. |
| 2   | ProcessIQ     | Excellent balance of operational focus and AI/intelligence capabilities. |
| 3   | ProcessPilot  | Conveys a helpful, guided, and interactive workforce assistant feel. |
| 4   | ProcessMind   | Strong alignment with institutional knowledge preservation and "operational brain" concepts. |
| 5   | ProcessOps | Modern, clean branding for enterprise operations and efficiency. |

## Example Branding Themes

### Theme 1: AI-Powered Operational Intelligence
> **"AI-Powered Operational Intelligence Platform"**
> Focuses heavily on the automated troubleshooting and diagnostic features of the system.

### Theme 2: Institutional Infrastructure
> **"The Operating System for Organizational Knowledge & Processes"**
> Focuses on centralizing all procedures and documentation into a single system of record.

### Theme 3: Workforce Enablement
> **"Workforce Intelligence for Process-Driven Organizations"**
> Focuses on wearable integrations, mobile support, and on-the-floor assistance for frontline workers.
